Argentina

Argentina is a country located in the most southern tip of the american continent. This country is home to about 46 million people, which more than 35% of them live in the area surrounding the capital, Buenos Aires, or in the capital itself. It's inhabitants are known for their "warm" manners and hospitality, but also for their passion of sports. This beautiful country will, despite all its errors, welcome all hard working people no matter ethnicity, social class or political views.
